What the research really means

The wildest exercise research I've found this week:

1. Quitting stair climbing is worse for your heart than never exercising at all (32% higher risk)

2. Athletes perform better on cognitive tests after pooping — researchers actually measured this

3. Just 200 steps twice a day increases your aerobic fitness by 17%

The point isn't that you need to work out in bathrooms (though apparently it helps?). It's that your body doesn't care WHERE you move. Stairs count. Walking to your car counts. Those weird places you'd never think of as "exercise" — they all count.

The Sardinians figured this out accidentally: they climb 30 flights of stairs daily just living in hilly towns with multi-story houses. No gym required.

So maybe we've been overthinking this whole fitness thing.
