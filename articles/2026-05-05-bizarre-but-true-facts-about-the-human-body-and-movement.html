Your body is weirder than you think:

  • Your eye muscle contracts in 1/100th of a second — fastest in your body

What the research really means

Your bones can support the weight of 4 pickup trucks in a space the size of a matchbox. Steel wishes it was that strong. 💪

Meanwhile, your eye muscle is out here blinking faster than you can process what just happened (literally 1/100th of a second).

And that "100 mph sneeze" everyone talks about? Total myth. Sneezes clock in at a measly 10 mph. Your cough is the real speed demon at 60 mph. 🌪️

The craziest part? Your entire skeleton replaces itself every decade. You're basically a walking ship of Theseus.

Your body is out here breaking physics and nobody talks about it enough.
