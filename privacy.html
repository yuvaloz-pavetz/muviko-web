Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Muviko ("we", "us", or "our") operates the website muviko.app and related services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you sign up for our email newsletter. This includes your email address.

We may also collect non-personal information automatically, such as browser type, device information, and pages visited, through cookies and similar technologies.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to send you our daily micro training research newsletter, improve our services, and communicate with you about updates and news from Muviko.

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

3. Instagram and Meta

We operate an Instagram account (@muviko.app) and use Meta's platform to publish content. When you interact with our Instagram content, Meta's own privacy policy applies to the data Meta collects.

We use Meta's APIs to publish content to our Instagram account. We do not collect or store personal data from Instagram users through these APIs.

4. Cookies

Our website may use cookies to enhance your experience. You can choose to disable cookies through your browser settings, though this may affect some functionality.

5. Data Retention

We retain your email address for as long as you are subscribed to our newsletter. You can unsubscribe at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in any of our emails.

6. Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the date at the top of this page.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: hello@muviko.app
